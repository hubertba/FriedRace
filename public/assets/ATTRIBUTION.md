# Visual Asset Attribution

The phase-one Fried Race visual upgrade contains no third-party vehicle models, textures, vegetation models, sky images, or prop assets.

All visual assets in this release are generated locally by the application from original Three.js geometry and deterministic procedural textures:

- unbranded `Sunset GT 1995` sports coupe;
- sky and late-afternoon lighting;
- grass and asphalt textures;
- trees, bushes, grass clumps, delineators, and streetlights;
- workshop garage and yard;
- road shoulders, markings, and building foundation details.

Map data remains © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License. See <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.
